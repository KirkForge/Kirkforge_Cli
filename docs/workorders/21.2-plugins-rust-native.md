> which there proposed *downgrading the claims*, here proposed *finishing the
> feature so the claims become true*)).** Where 20.0.9 leaned toward
> de-scoping, WO 21 leans toward completion. The cut/spin-off option still
> exists as a decision in 21.1-R1/R2, but the default direction is
> keep-and-finish. If 21.1 flips a crate to cut, the corresponding item here is
> voided by that decision (recorded, not silently dropped).
