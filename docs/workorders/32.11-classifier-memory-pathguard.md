# Workorder 32.11 — Three Deferrals from WO 29.3

## Status: Pending

## Problem

Three loose ends left by the WO 29.3 pure-module port batch:

1. **ClassifierMemory learned-examples** —
   `crates/kf-routing/src/classifier.rs:5` says "no persistence
   (ClassifierMemory learned-examples is deferred to WO 29.6)" but
   WO 29.6 shipped without it. The comment now points at a closed WO;
   the deferral is untracked.

2. **buildCorrectionPrompt real template** —
   `crates/kf-routing/src/correction.rs` uses a placeholder prompt.
   WO 29.7 was supposed to swap in the real one; it didn't.

3. **PathGuard unification** — `src/session/access/mod.rs` PathGuard
   (async, config-coupled) and `kf-routing::path_safety` (pure, sync)
   are two implementations of the same path-safety concept living in
   different layers with different signatures.

## Root cause

Each is a deferral that outlived its parent WO without being
re-tracked. (1) and (2) are pure "do the thing" items; (3) is a
duplication that grew because the port batch extracted the pure module
without collapsing the older async impl.

## Approach

1. Decide on ClassifierMemory learned-examples: either implement
   persistence (serialize the learned set to the session dir) or
   explicitly disclose the deferral to a new WO and update the
   `classifier.rs:5` comment to point at it. Do not leave it pointing
   at closed WO 29.6.
2. Swap the placeholder `buildCorrectionPrompt` template for the real
   one, or disclose the deferral with a concrete blocker + new WO.
3. Unify the two PathGuard impls behind one signature. Keep the pure
   sync version in `kf-routing::path_safety` as the source of truth;
   have the async `session::access` version delegate to it.

## Files to touch

- `crates/kf-routing/src/classifier.rs`
- `crates/kf-routing/src/correction.rs`
- `src/session/access/mod.rs`

## Gate

## Done condition

All three items are either resolved or explicitly disclosed with a
concrete blocker, the exact remaining work, and a tracking WO. No
comment points at a closed WO as if it were still pending. No silent
deferral.
