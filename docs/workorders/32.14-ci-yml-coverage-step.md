# Workorder 32.14 — WO 28.7 R4 — CI YAML Coverage Regression Step

## Status: Pending

## Problem

WO 28.7 R4 deferred wiring the coverage-regression gate into CI.
`scripts/check-cov-regression.sh` exists and works locally
(`scripts/ci-local.sh` calls it), but the CI YAML
(`.github/workflows/ci.yml`) does NOT invoke it. The gate is live
locally and dead in CI — a regression in coverage can merge if no one
runs `ci-local.sh` before push.

## Root cause

The script was written and validated locally but the CI job was never
updated to call it. Pure CI-YAML gap, no code change required.

## Approach

1. Add a step to the relevant CI job in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`
   that runs `bash scripts/check-cov-regression.sh` after the test
   step (it needs the coverage artifacts produced by the test run).
2. Ensure the step runs in the same job that produces coverage data,
   or that artifacts are passed between jobs.
3. Make the step gating (continue-on-error vs hard-fail) match the
   local behavior in `ci-local.sh` — do not silently downgrade it.

## Files to touch

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`

## Gate

- CI runs the coverage regression step (verify on a PR that pushes the
  change)

## Done condition

The `ci.yml` coverage job calls `bash scripts/check-cov-regression.sh`.
The step fails the job on a coverage regression (not silent). Local
and CI behavior match.
