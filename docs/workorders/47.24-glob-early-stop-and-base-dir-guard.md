# WO 47 — Glob: max_matches never early-stops the walk + base_dir not pre-guarded

## Status: Planned

## Problem

HIGH for large repos. src/tools/glob.rs: the walker traverses the ENTIRE tree, collects every match into a Vec, and only then truncates to max_matches — {"max_matches":1} on a 100K-match repo buffers 100K paths then throws them away. Also: the walker starts from base_dir BEFORE any path_guard check — base_dir="/" pattern="**/*" walks the filesystem broadly and filters per-entry afterward (resource-control bypass; returned paths stay policy-clean but the traversal cost is unguarded). Fix: (1) stop traversal (or stop retaining) once max_matches is reached — report 'Found at least N; showing N'; (2) path_guard.check_traversal(base_path) before spawning the walker; keep the per-entry check for symlink races. Fold in the GPT-#9 residual: the blocking walk is raced against cancel (46.8) but keeps running — early-stop bounds it.
