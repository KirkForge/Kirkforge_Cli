1. **mm-H35 — e2e theater (DONE).** Both scenarios now pin their named
   behavior on the wire log + exit status + relayed text.
   `auto_approve_skips_approval`: exit 0, "Cleanup done." relayed, exactly

   executed tool's result — not the `❌ Approval denied: …` reason the

2. **mm-H36 — requires_model flags (DONE, scope narrower than the audit's
   count).** Classified all 20 unflagged TOMLs by whether `verify_only()`
   (which runs the verify against the *unedited* setup) passes. 5 genuinely
   need a live model and now carry `requires_model = true`:
   add_doc_comment (no `///` in setup), add_adr (ADR-062 file absent),
   add_enum_variant (no "Pending"), fix_failing_test (assertion says 5),
   fix_clippy_warning (`new_without_default` fires under `-D warnings`).
   The other 15 were left unflagged on purpose: their verifies pass on the
   unedited setup (grep-for-existing-content checks or already-green
   `cargo test`/`cargo clippy` setups), so they are genuine verify-only
   tasks and flagging them would silently skip verifiable tasks. The
   audit's "20 of 30" counted flag *absence*, not flag-worthiness.
   Observation (out of scope, not fixed here): several of those 15 have
   verifies that grep for pre-rename/pre-refactor content — they pass
   before the model runs and would fail after a *correct* edit (e.g.
   rename_function greps for `pub fn old_name`). That is verify-correctness
   theater of a different kind; follow-up candidate, tracked here.
3. **mm-H33 — tarpaulin per-directory gate (VERDICT: present claim wrong,
   absence claim right; documented, no CI change).** A coverage job DOES
   exist in `.github/workflows/ci-nightly.yml` (`coverage` job, ending in
   the `Coverage regression gate` step) — so kimi's "the coverage gate
   exists" is true at that level. But it is NOT ADR-065's gate: it is the
   WO 28.7 per-CRATE llvm-cov baseline gate
   (`cargo llvm-cov --workspace --lcov` + `scripts/check-cov-regression.sh`
   vs `docs/coverage-baseline.md`). ADR-065's tarpaulin per-DIRECTORY gate
   (`src/session: 68.5, src/tools: 76.0, src/adapters: 75.0`) is in no CI
   workflow — mm-H33 is right about that. History: ADR-065's gate was born
   in `ci.yml` (WO 12.9, 6bb4cc30); WO 28.7 (4c01e5b2, 2026-08-13)
   deliberately replaced the CI coverage job's tarpaulin gate with the
   llvm-cov per-crate gate and moved the per-directory enforcement to
   `scripts/ci-local.sh full` (`enforce_coverage_thresholds`, where the
   kf-testdoctor guard `default_thresholds_match_local_gate` now pins it);
   the WO 32c split (cd79462d) dropped nothing further — the pre-split
   ci.yml already ran the llvm-cov gate. Reconciliation: two gates at
   different granularity — per-directory tarpaulin (local full gate),
   per-crate llvm-cov (CI nightly). Restoring the tarpaulin gate to CI on
   top of the existing nightly coverage job would double a 30-min job and
   reintroduce the documented tarpaulin flakiness that motivated WO 28.7.
   Follow-up (out of this WO's scope, shared docs): ADR-065's Context
   sentence "The CI coverage gate lives in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`
   (`Enforce coverage gate` step)" is stale since WO 28.7 and should be
   amended to name both gates and where each lives.
